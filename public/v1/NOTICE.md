# RollPig Resource Attribution Notice

This resource candidate is intentionally built using a conservative
source-first / rights-first policy.

## Project/resource lineage

The included resources are limited to items with machine-verifiable provenance
in one of the following categories:

1. Exact upstream resources from:
   - Bearlele/nonebot-plugin-rollpig
   - MegSopern/astrbot_plugin_rollpig

2. Exact historical resources from:
   - Felis2026/nonebot-plugin-rollpig-plus

   Only resources whose exact user-facing record and exact image bytes were
   verified as already present in the RollPig Plus repository before the later
   rollpig-resources resource-license introduction are included in this
   candidate.

The historical RollPig Plus snapshot used for that verification was licensed
under MIT and preserved the copyright notices of Bear_lele and Felis.

## Excluded resources

Resources are NOT included when:

- their current redistribution rights cannot be established;
- they only appear in the later Felis resource snapshot without proof of
  earlier MIT publication;
- they are community submissions without an explicit stored redistribution
  permission;
- their source is unknown.

Those resources remain quarantined and are not part of this candidate.
